# Which models complement Claude as prompt critics — results (2026-10-01)

**Question.** Given a prompt artifact with known defects, which non-Anthropic models catch what
Claude misses, at what cost? Pre-registered in
`bogheorghiu/personal-use-tools` → `prompt-engineering/battery/PREREG-prompt-engineering-vs-directory-2026-10-01.md`
(section "Complement battery"). Direct OpenRouter API measurement — not an Ori eval.

**Instrument.**
- `artifacts.json`: 8 prompt artifacts (system prompts, graders, a skill) written by Fable 5.1
  with 61 planted defects (obvious / moderate / subtle) and 21 decoys (things that look wrong
  but are justified by the stated context). A separate Opus agent verified each defect and decoy
  against the text and added unplanted genuine defects to the key.
- One identical review prompt (`lib/critic-run.mjs`), one rep per (artifact, model), reasoning
  effort low on OpenRouter, `--effort low` for Claude via headless `claude -p`.
- Matching: `match-critiques.js` — an Opus agent per artifact labels every finding of every
  critic as planted (which defect) / decoy / valid-unplanted / invalid, with critics anonymised
  and shuffled (`lib/match-prep.mjs`). Two artifacts were matched twice independently: **matcher
  agreement 0.966** (225/233 findings labelled identically).
- Scoring: `lib/critic-score.mjs`. Raw critiques, labels and the anonymisation key are in this
  folder.

Opus alone found 51/61; all four Claude critics together 54; all 16 critics 57. Four defects were
found by nobody (one rated "obvious" by its author — possibly a key error, not checked).
`mistralai/mistral-medium-3-5` returned empty content on 8 of 9 calls (every token spent in
hidden reasoning at a 14k cap, $0.89 total) and is excluded: unusable at this setting.

## What it supports

- **A second, non-Claude critic is worth adding to an Opus pass, and GPT-6.1 Sol is the best
  single addition measured** — it found more of what Opus missed (+4) than Fable did (+3), at about
  a cent per review. Gemini 3.8 Flash and GLM-5.3 are the cheap complements (+2 each, under a
  cent).
- **Fable 5.1 is the most precise critic here** (0.97, one decoy hit): use it where false alarms
  are expensive. Opus has the highest recall but flags more non-problems (18 invalid findings).
- **Expensive is not better here**: Gemini Pro (latest) and Qwen 3.8 Max added nothing over Opus.

## What it cannot see

- One rep per cell and 61 defects: the "+N" margins are 1–4 defects, so the ranking among the
  +2 group is noise; only "GPT-6.1 Sol and Grok 4.7 lead, the bottom three add nothing" is
  read from it.
- Synthetic artifacts written by Fable: a critic that shares Fable's notion of a defect may be
  favoured. Claude critics in particular may share the author's priors.
- Reasoning effort was low for everyone; rankings at higher effort may differ.
- Matching was done by one Opus agent per artifact; agreement 0.966 on the two re-matched
  artifacts bounds but does not eliminate that noise.
